Edge AI Hardware Selection for Embedded Products

A device with a larger accelerator may still fail if the model runtime is weak, camera pipeline is unstable, memory bandwidth is limited, heat cannot escape the enclosure, or firmware updates cannot safely replace the model in the field. For embedded products, edge AI hardware should be selected from the workload outward.
